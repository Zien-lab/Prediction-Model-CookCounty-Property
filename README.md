# 🧠 Predictive Modeling for Property Valuation (R Language)

**📅 Duration**: Nov 2024 – Dec 2024  
**🏢 Organization**: Cook County Assessor’s Office (Capstone Project)

## 📌 Project Overview

Led a team of three to develop predictive models for estimating property values in Cook County. The project aimed to support public property assessments through accurate and interpretable modeling.

### 🔧 Methodology

- Confirmed and compared three modeling approaches:
  - **Linear Regression**
  - **Lasso Regression**
  - **Random Forest**
- Assigned modeling responsibilities among team members and iteratively improved results through collaborative discussion and tuning.

### 🏆 Key Achievements

- **Selected Random Forest** as the final model for its superior performance.
- Utilized feature importance scores to identify **32 key predictors**.
- Achieved **4th-highest prediction accuracy** out of 100 teams on unseen future datasets.
- Leveraged R packages: `ranger`, `randomForest`, and `doParallel` for efficient training and parallel processing.

## 🛠 Skills & Tools

- **Programming Language**: R
- **Techniques**: Machine Learning, Feature Selection, Model Evaluation
- **Libraries**: `ranger`, `randomForest`, `doParallel`
- **Additional**: Data Visualization, Collaborative Modeling, Data Analysis
